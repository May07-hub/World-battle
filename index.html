LET THE WORLD DECIDE.

World Battle is a global platform where people gather around one question and choose between two sides.

01

A QUESTION

Every battle begins with a clear question that can be answered by two sides.

02

THE PEOPLE

Anyone eligible to participate can cast their vote during the battle.

03

SEVEN DAYS

The battle remains open for seven days. Then voting closes.

04

THE VERDICT

The final public result becomes the official World Verdict.

PARTICIPANT CHARTER

THE RULES OF THE COURT

  • Every vote should represent a real person.
  • Do not manipulate, threaten or harass other voters.
  • Do not use bots or fraudulent accounts.
  • Every participant may form their own opinion.
  • The World Verdict represents the public vote, not absolute truth.
